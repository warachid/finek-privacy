Finek Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Finek ("the app," "we," "us") is a mobile app that lets you ping a friend and have them send back their location (and optionally a photo), and plan meetings with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

Finek is developed and operated by Rachid, an independent developer based in Morocco, who is the data controller for the personal data processed by the app. Contact: rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, you choose a username and password. Your username is visible to other users so friends can find and add you. Your password is stored securely by Firebase Authentication (Google) — we never see or store it in plain text.

Recovery email (optional). You may add a recovery email address when creating your account, but this is entirely optional and not required to use the app. It is used only if you request a password reset, and only that reset flow can access it — it is never shown to other users and never used for any other purpose.

Location data. The app uses your device's precise location only while you are using it, and never tracks you in the background:

  • Answering a FINEK: when you tap to answer a friend's FINEK request, your location is sent to that specific friend.
  • Checking in to a meeting: when you tap "I'm here", the app checks your location against the meeting place to confirm you have arrived. Only the fact that you checked in and the time are saved and shown to the other people in that meeting — your coordinates are not stored or shared.
  • Maps: when you open a meeting or choose a meeting place, the app uses your location on your own phone to show where you are on the map and how far you are from the place. This is not sent to us or to your friends.

Camera and photos. The app requests camera access so you can take a photo when answering a FINEK request, and photo library access if you choose to set a profile picture. Photos are stored as part of your account/ping data as described below.

Meetings. When you create a meeting, the app stores its name, date and time, the meeting place you chose (its name and map coordinates), the friends you invited, and each person's check-in time once they arrive. This is visible only to the people in that meeting. Invited friends can leave a meeting at any time, which removes them from it.

Place search. When you search for a meeting place, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's search service (Nominatim) to find matching places. Do not type personal information into the search.

Profile links. If you choose to share your profile, the link contains your username, so anyone you send it to can see your username and open your profile in the app.

Friends and social data. The app stores your friends list, pending friend requests, and any users you've chosen to block, so these features can work.

Push notification token. To notify you of friend requests, FINEK pings and meeting updates, the app stores a device push token (provided by Expo's push notification service) linked to your account.

Advertising data. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data to serve and measure ads. See Google's ad policy and AdMob's data disclosure for details on what Google collects and how it's used.

How Photos and Locations Are Handled

A photo and location sent in response to a FINEK request are visible to the requesting friend only, and are automatically and permanently deleted as soon as that friend views them (or closes the screen). They are not kept, archived, or used for any other purpose.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and storage of account and app data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications. See Expo's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — serves in-app ads. See links above.
  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation) — provides the map images and the place search used for meetings. Your device connects to their servers to load the map, which lets them see your IP address and the area of the map being viewed. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • unpkg — delivers the map software (Leaflet) to the app when a map is opened; it receives your IP address as part of that request.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Where Your Data Is Stored (Transfers Outside Morocco)

Finek does not run its own servers. Your data is stored and processed by the following providers, outside Morocco:

  • Google Firebase — account data, friends, FINEK requests and answers are stored in a Firestore database located in Belgium (European Union). Login is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Expo (650 Industries, United States) — receives your push notification token and the notification text in order to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google AdMob (Google, United States and other countries) — receives your advertising identifier and technical device data to show ads.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom, servers in several countries) — receives your IP address, the map area you view, and any place search you type, in order to show maps and search results.
  • unpkg (served through Cloudflare, United States and other countries) — receives your IP address when the map software is loaded.

These transfers are carried out in accordance with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data. Belgium is on the list of countries recognised by the CNDP as providing adequate protection. For transfers to the United States and the United Kingdom, we ask for your explicit consent when you create your account — you cannot create an account without accepting this policy.

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account data (username, profile photo, friends, settings, recovery email): until you delete your account.
  • Locations and photos sent in response to a FINEK: deleted as soon as your friend views them.
  • Meetings (name, time, place, members and check-in times): kept until the organizer deletes the meeting. If you leave a meeting, your name and check-in are removed from it.
  • Your consent record (date and policy version): kept with your account as proof of consent.

Your Rights (Law 09-08)

Under Moroccan Law No. 09-08 you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to object to its processing, and to have it deleted. To exercise these rights, email rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com from your recovery email or mention your username. We will reply as quickly as possible. You may also contact the Moroccan data protection authority, the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

This processing is being declared to the CNDP.

Data Deletion

You can remove friends or block users at any time from within the app. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us using the email below — we will process deletion requests within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Finek is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard services (Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules) to restrict access to your data to you and, where the app's features require it, your friends. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
