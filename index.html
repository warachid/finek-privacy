Finek Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Finek ("the app," "we," "us") is a mobile app that lets you ping a friend and have them send back their location (and optionally a photo), and plan meetings with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

Finek is developed and operated by Rachid, an independent developer based in Morocco, who is the data controller for the personal data processed by the app. Contact: rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, you choose a username and password. Your username and profile picture or avatar are visible to other signed-in users who look you up by your exact username, so friends can find and add you. Nobody can browse or list the users of the app. Your friends list is visible only to you and your friends. Your password is stored securely by Firebase Authentication (Google) — we never see or store it in plain text.

Recovery email (optional). You may add a recovery email address when creating your account, but this is entirely optional and not required to use the app. Once you verify it, it also becomes the email your account signs in with — from then on you log in with that email instead of your username — and the app uses it only for logging in and password resets. It is never shown to other users, listed, published, or used for any other purpose.

Location data. The app uses your device's precise location only while you are using it, and never tracks you in the background:

  • Answering a FINEK: when you tap to answer a friend's FINEK request, your location is sent to that specific friend.
  • Checking in to a meeting: when you tap "I'm here", the app checks your location against the meeting place to confirm you have arrived. Only the fact that you checked in and the time are saved and shown to the other people in that meeting — your coordinates are not stored or shared.
  • Maps: when you open a meeting or choose a meeting place, the app uses your location on your own phone to show where you are on the map and how far you are from the place. This is not sent to us or to your friends.

Camera and photos. The app requests camera access so you can take a photo when answering a FINEK request, and photo library access if you choose to set a profile picture. Photos are stored as part of your account/ping data as described below.

Meetings. When you create a meeting, the app stores its name, date and time, the meeting place you chose (its name and map coordinates), the friends you invited, and each person's check-in time once they arrive. This is visible only to the people in that meeting. Invited friends can leave a meeting at any time, which removes them from it.

Place search. When you search for a meeting place, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's search service (Nominatim) to find matching places. Do not type personal information into the search.

Profile links. If you choose to share your profile, the link contains your username, so anyone you send it to can see your username and open your profile in the app.

Friends and social data. The app stores your friends list, pending friend requests, and any users you've chosen to block, so these features can work. Your blocked list is visible only to you.

Push notification token. To notify you of FINEK pings, new messages and meeting updates from your friends, the app stores a device push token (provided by Expo's push notification service) linked to your account. Only you and your friends can read it, so only friends can send you notifications. New friend requests are shown in the app, without a notification.

Advertising data. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data to serve and measure ads. See Google's ad policy and AdMob's data disclosure for details on what Google collects and how it's used. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Union), the app asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.

How Photos and Locations Are Handled

A photo and location sent in response to a FINEK request are visible to the requesting friend only, and are automatically and permanently deleted as soon as that friend views them (or closes the screen). They are not kept, archived, or used for any other purpose.

Messages

You can send short text messages to your friends in the app. Only friends can message each other, and blocked users cannot send or receive messages.

  • Who can see them: a message is visible in the app only to you and the friend you sent it to.
  • They disappear after 5 minutes: each message disappears from the app 5 minutes after it is sent. It is then deleted from our database (Google Firebase) the next time you or your friend open the app. Until then, an expired message stays stored but is never shown again.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted: messages are protected in transit and by Firebase security rules, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. While a message is stored, it is technically accessible to the developer through the Firebase console and to Google as our hosting provider. We do not read, use or share message content.
  • "Seen": when you open a chat, your friend can see that you have read their messages. For this, the app stores only the time up to which you have read that chat — never any message text.
  • "Typing…": while you type in a chat, your friend sees that you are typing. Only a timestamp is stored for this — never what you type — and it is deleted when you send the message or leave the chat.
  • Unsend: you can delete one of your own messages for both of you before it disappears on its own.
  • Screenshots: the person you message can take a screenshot or copy a message before it disappears. Only send what you are comfortable sharing.
  • Notifications: when you receive a message, the notification shows the sender's username and "New message" only — never the text of the message.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and storage of account and app data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications. See Expo's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — serves in-app ads. See links above.
  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation) — provides the map images and the place search used for meetings. Your device connects to their servers to load the map, which lets them see your IP address and the area of the map being viewed. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • unpkg — delivers the map software (Leaflet) to the app when a map is opened; it receives your IP address as part of that request.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Where Your Data Is Stored (Transfers Outside Morocco)

Finek does not run its own servers. Your data is stored and processed by the following providers, outside Morocco:

  • Google Firebase — account data, friends, FINEK requests and answers, and messages are stored in a Firestore database located in Belgium (European Union). Login is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Expo (650 Industries, United States) — receives your push notification token and the notification text in order to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google AdMob (Google, United States and other countries) — receives your advertising identifier and technical device data to show ads.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom, servers in several countries) — receives your IP address, the map area you view, and any place search you type, in order to show maps and search results.
  • unpkg (served through Cloudflare, United States and other countries) — receives your IP address when the map software is loaded.

These transfers are carried out in accordance with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data. Belgium is on the list of countries recognised by the CNDP as providing adequate protection. For transfers to the United States and the United Kingdom, we ask for your explicit consent when you create your account — you cannot create an account without accepting this policy.

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account data (username, profile photo, friends, settings, recovery email): until you delete your account.
  • Locations and photos sent in response to a FINEK: deleted as soon as your friend views them.
  • Messages: hidden 5 minutes after they are sent, and deleted from the database the next time you or your friend open the app.
  • Meetings (name, time, place, members and check-in times): kept until the organizer deletes the meeting. If you leave a meeting, your name and check-in are removed from it.
  • Your consent record (date and policy version): kept with your account as proof of consent.

Your Rights (Law 09-08)

Under Moroccan Law No. 09-08 you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to object to its processing, and to have it deleted. You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, friends, messages, FINEKs and meetings, and frees your username. To exercise your other rights, or if you can no longer open the app, email rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com from your recovery email or mention your username. We will reply as quickly as possible. You may also contact the Moroccan data protection authority, the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

This processing is being declared to the CNDP.

Data Deletion

Delete your account in the app: open Finek and go to Profile → Delete my account, then confirm by entering your password. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data — your profile, profile photo, friends and friend requests, blocked list, messages, FINEKs, meetings and recovery email — and frees your username. This cannot be undone.

Can't open the app? Email rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com from your recovery email, or mention your username, and ask for your account to be deleted. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also remove friends or block users at any time from within the app.

Children's Privacy

Finek is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard services (Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules) to restrict access to your data to you and, where the app's features require it, your friends. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.
